Training Programs

Body

Personal Training

Train directly with studio owner Master Sunny Fong. With over 35 years of dedicated fitness experience, Sunny designs customized exercise routines tailored to your goals. You will achieve progressive, measurable results in a safe and supportive environment.

Energy

Tai Chi

A traditional Chinese martial art created to maximize chi (internal energy). Regular practice of the Yang style develops flexibility, physical strength, balance, and a profound sense of inner calm.

Power

Taekwondo

A dynamic Korean martial art featuring explosive kicking and punching. Emphasizes self-defense, Taeguek forms, and character development through the five tenets: courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, and indomitable spirit.

Mind

Zhong Chi Chuan

An internal martial art developed by Master Fong. Combining slow and fast, soft and hard techniques, this holistic practice develops mental clarity, overall balance, and whole-body strength for adults.

About Sun Training Center

Decades of Expertise in Fitness & Martial Arts

Master Sunny Fong

Master Sunny Fong has been a leader in the health and fitness industry for over 35 years. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Health & Fitness and a Master's degree in Exercise Science.

Sunny is a 6th-Degree Black Belt in Taekwondo, certified by the World Taekwondo Federation. While attending graduate school, he studied Yang-style Tai Chi under Grand Master Lijun Cheng during a dedicated Qi Gong research study, and has been teaching the art for 30 years.
